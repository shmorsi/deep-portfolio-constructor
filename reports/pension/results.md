# Pension Lab: results

Generated by `python -m dpc.cli pension-site` from `reports/pension/summary.json` (commit 304f7c8). Data: 882 months, 1953-04 to 2026-09. A plan 90% funded, 60 years of indexed payments (duration 14.2 years at 4%), followed for 20 years. Costs are what the sponsor pays in plus any shortfall left at the end, per $100 promised, in start-of-plan money. The score is the average cost plus the average cost of the worst 5%. Do not edit by hand.

## The scenario generator and the training

- Yield: AR(1) with coefficient 0.9939 a month around 5.98%. Inflation: AR(1) with coefficient 0.545. Shocks are resampled from history in blocks averaging 12 months.
- 24 networks of 1,507 parameters each (3 seeds for the simulated tests and for each of the walk-forward refits), 300 steps of 1,024 scenarios, 8,192 training and 4,096 validation scenarios per network.
